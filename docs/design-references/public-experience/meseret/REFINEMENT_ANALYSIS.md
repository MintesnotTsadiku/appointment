## Imagery and representation

Use six distinct scenes: shutter opening, shop bookkeeping, business-starting still life, adviser conversation, preparation still life, and online meeting. Add an Addis office/city scene where needed for the final pair.

The reference shows a male shopkeeper from behind, a female bookkeeper from behind, and a female/male adviser pair facing a customer whose back is visible. Follow those scene roles. No invented industry-wide demographic ratio is necessary. Staff may face the camera. Customers remain turned away.

The current two-person photograph does not clearly identify both people and is repeated in two sections. Replace that ambiguity with consistent adviser portraits within the consultation scene. Keep the existing Rahel Abebe provider identity and calendar. A second adviser, if included as authored staff, must not silently create another bookable calendar.

Photographic notebooks and signage may carry atmosphere. Essential service details, adviser names, preparation guidance and booking actions must remain real HTML text. Generated lettering must not become required information.

## Necessary platform adaptations

- Keep the existing three offerings, IDs, prices and durations unless an explicit content change is approved.
- Use a real shadcn service selector in the peach booking band. Continue to the shared scheduler for live format, date, time and customer details. Do not reproduce the reference's illustrative date/time buttons as fake availability.
- Use Africa/Addis_Ababa, UTC+3. The reference's UTC−3 label is an error. The scene labels 09:00, 12:00 and 16:00 tell a story; they do not promise available appointments.
- Use the existing seeded Bole address and business hours. Do not copy a reference address or schedule merely to match the pixels.
- Preserve owner-controlled live availability and holiday greeting, with a quiet treatment that fits the page. Keep Habesha hours.
- Translate the content and booking handoff. Allow Amharic to wrap naturally. Do not force Latin italics or fixed English heights onto Ethiopic text.
- Preserve the phone speed budget, keyboard access, AA contrast, reduced motion, and truthful service claims.

## Expression that fits the reference

The existing `imagery: mixed`, `motion: calm`, `darkMode: tonal`, `holiday: greeting` and `success: static` choices fit. The greeting should move from the separate header strip into the hero, so its declaration should change from `header` to `hero` during implementation.

Motion should stay quiet: precise link and control feedback and restrained scene reveals through the kit. No simulated moving shutter or extra decorative animation is needed. Loading can echo a paper spread with image and text placeholders. Success can be a carefully typeset appointment entry with a small ledger/check mark. The booking skin should continue the paper/plum palette, serif hierarchy and thin rules.

Dark mode should use warm ink/plum surfaces and readable cream text, with deliberate image treatment. It should retain the scene structure. No new night photographs are required by the tonal declaration. The welcome sprig belongs in the booking band because it appears in the reference; it is not a reason to add decoration elsewhere.
