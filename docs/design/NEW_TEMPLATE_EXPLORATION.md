## Hana Bekele

Hana hosts an Addis-based culture and everyday-life podcast. She shares longer conversations on YouTube and clips on TikTok and Instagram.
Her audience includes Ethiopian professionals and diaspora listeners. A producer sometimes helps with scheduling.

| Offering | Duration | Estimated fee | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conversation on the visitor's topic | 20 min | 1,000–2,000 | Video |
| Podcast or content feedback | 45 min | 3,000–5,000 | Video |
| Coffee and conversation | 60 min | 4,000–7,000 | Selected Addis venue |
| Lunch with Hana | 90 min | 7,000–12,000 | Selected restaurant. State whether the meal is included. |
| Collaboration discussion | 30 min | Free, by request | Video |

The visitor recognizes Hana, sees selected work, chooses an offering, enters a topic, and selects a time.
Paid offerings need a clear payment and confirmation flow. A conversation does not include an endorsement or published appearance.
Predictable sessions can use instant booking. Lunches and collaborations may require approval, subject to product support.
Start the showcase with conversations, feedback, and coffee. Offer lunch in limited windows.

Paid creator campaigns exist locally. Demand for paid personal conversations and meals remains a hypothesis.
This template can also serve authors, speakers, and other public personalities.

## Dr. Dawit Tesfaye

Dawit is an internal-medicine specialist focused on ongoing adult care. He works at a public hospital and a private hospital.
His independent practice offers scheduled consultations and selected home visits. His employers do not need platform accounts.
One assistant handles scheduling. Dawit controls clinical decisions and appointment suitability.

| Offering | Duration | Estimated fee | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scheduled follow-up | 20 min | 1,000–1,800 | Video |
| Review of existing results and care questions | 40 min | 2,000–3,500 | Video, when clinically appropriate |
| Patient and family care discussion | 45 min | 2,000–3,500 | Video, with patient consent |
| Planned home consultation | 60 min plus travel | 4,000–7,000 | Defined Addis service area |

Example schedule, using the international clock:

- Weekdays 08:00–13:00: unavailable because of public-hospital work.
- Monday, Wednesday, Friday 14:00–17:00: unavailable because of private-hospital work.
- Tuesday and Thursday 15:00–18:00: independent video appointments.
- Saturday 09:00–12:00: limited home visits with travel buffers.
- Leave and other exceptions close affected slots.

Visitors see available appointments, not private calendar details. Manual rules can establish this model without hospital integration.
Automatic external-calendar synchronization is a separate capability to verify.
The page covers scheduled care and gives an urgent-care notice. Verify applicable professional requirements before real deployment.
Review consent and health-information handling. Cross-border medical care requires separate review.
A relative abroad paying for care in Ethiopia is a different scenario from treating a patient abroad.

## Product checks before implementation

The independent showcase already demonstrates online and in-person offerings. This does not prove every proposed workflow exists.

- Verify availability rules, exceptions, and travel buffers.
- Verify assistant permissions and access boundaries.
- Verify payment, cancellation, and refund behavior.
- Verify topic fields, intake, and approval requests.
- Verify meeting-link creation and visibility.
- Verify distinctions between patient, booking contact, payer, and participant.

Record any missing capability as separate product work. Do not hide it inside a visual template change.
