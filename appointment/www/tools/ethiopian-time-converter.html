Convert Ethiopian (Habesha) time to international time (East Africa Time) and back. Habesha clock hours start at sunrise, so 6:00 AM is 12 o'clock (ጠዋት 12 ሰዓት).

The rule: Habesha hour = international hour − 6, on a 12-hour clock. Minutes do not change. The day (ቀን) runs from 6:00 AM to 5:59 PM, and the night (ሌሊት) from 6:00 PM to 5:59 AM.
